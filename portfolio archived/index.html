  𓄲 𓏼 About Me .⋆♱  

Hello (world), I am just another artist navigating the world through my own set of glasses. I try to find various mediums that I have a passion for, to express myself. Aside from self-expression, I believe art is a big player in communication. Art has endless room for imagination and creativity, while also maintaining its original function no matter how discrete. As an artist I hope to emphasize that words alone are not the only way to communicate. The realm of colors, shapes, and textures can break through various barriers that exist in language. We are all human at the end of the day, and we have emotions that can be evoked for better understanding of each other.
